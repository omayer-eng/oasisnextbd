VenGuard / Our story

Our story

We want a gas leak to be handled without anyone having to be awake or come running.

Why we exist

Millions of kitchens in Bangladesh use LPG cylinders. Most have no protection, and where there is some, an ordinary alarm only makes noise. We set out to build something that acts by itself, without relying on power, the internet or anyone being there.

Our mission

To bring affordable safety that acts by itself to every kitchen.
