VenGuard / Privacy policy

Privacy policy

Draft — awaiting review by our lawyer. Last updated: October 2026.

What we collect

  • What you enter in our forms: name, mobile number, email (optional), area and district, kitchen type and number, cylinder brand, dealer code.
  • Payment status and transaction reference. We never see or store your card number or bKash/Nagad PIN — the payment provider handles them.
  • If you agree: statistics about how the site is used (pages viewed, device, city), via cookies.

Why

To contact you about your pre-order or enquiry, to take and refund deposits, to deliver and install, and to improve the site.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not send your name, phone number or email to analytics tools (Google Analytics, Meta, Clarity).

Where it is kept

In VenGuard's own database, which only authorised team members can access. We use trusted providers to process payments and send SMS and email; they receive only what they need for that task.

Your choices

Ask us to see, correct or delete your data at any time. You can change your cookie choice from “Cookie settings” in the footer.
