VenGuard / VenGuard Home kit

VenGuard Home kit

For your home kitchen. It senses gas, shuts the gas off by itself, and you reset it in 10 seconds.

What is in the kit

  • Gas detector

    Sits on the kitchen wall and senses gas in the air.

  • Automatic valve

    Fits on the gas line and shuts off gas when a leak is confirmed.

  • Batteries

    So it runs without mains power.

Every part, up close

How it installs

No cutting or changing gas pipes, no special tools.

  1. 1

    Turn off the cylinder

    Switch the regulator off.

  2. 2

    Fit the valve

    Follow the picture guide to fit the valve on the gas line.

  3. 3

    Place the detector and test

    Mount the detector and press the test button.

Prefer not to do it yourself? Our team or an authorised dealer will install it.
