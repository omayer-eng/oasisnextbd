VenGuard / How it works

How it works

Four steps. None of them needs mains power, the internet or anyone at home — that is how it is designed.

  1. 1. Sensing

    The detector checks the kitchen air for LPG all the time. LPG is heavier than air, so the detector is mounted low.

  2. 2. The 7-second check

    So that a brief whiff while cooking does not cause a false alarm, VenGuard watches the level for 7 seconds to confirm it is a real leak.

  3. 3. Closing the valve

    Once confirmed, the valve shuts off gas from the cylinder. It is designed to close in under 1 second.

    Testing in progress

  4. 4. Manual reset

    The valve stays closed until a person resets it. Open the windows to air the room, then reset it in about 10 seconds. This is deliberate, so gas never turns back on by itself.

What VenGuard does not do

The website never controls a valve

Protection happens entirely in the device.

It never turns gas back on by itself

A person always does the reset.

It does not depend on a subscription
