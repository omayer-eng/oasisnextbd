FAQ

Answers to common questions

Does it work in a blackout?

Yes, that is how it is designed. VenGuard runs on batteries, so sensing and closing the valve are meant to keep working when the power goes. This is being tested now and we will publish the results here.

Does it need Wi-Fi?

No. The core protection happens inside the device and does not need the internet. Only the optional Connected plan uses the internet, for phone alerts and remote close.

Is it safe to install myself?

VenGuard is designed so you do not cut or change gas pipes and need no special tools. You will get step-by-step picture instructions. If you are unsure, our team or an authorised dealer can install it for you.

What happens after it closes the valve?

The gas stays off and the detector's light shows that the valve has closed. Open the windows and do not use switches or flames. Once the area is safe, reset it by hand; it takes about 10 seconds.

What does it cost?

You can pre-order now with a refundable BDT 500 deposit. The final Home kit price will be announced soon, and your deposit comes off that price. The optional Connected plan is BDT 550 a year.

How do I get a refund of the deposit?

Tell us any time before delivery by WhatsApp, phone or email, with your order number. We send the money back the way you paid. See the refund policy for details.

Which cylinders does it fit?

We are designing VenGuard for the household LPG cylinders and regulators common in Bangladesh. We will publish a confirmed list of brands after testing. Tell us your cylinder brand in the pre-order form and we will check it for you.
